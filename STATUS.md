## Kitchen planner + gait personalization (branch `wm-planner`, 2026-10-24, not merged to demo-safe)
- **Planner** (`planner/`, `viewer/src/planner.ts`, `planfix.ts`, `planworker.ts`). A layout is chair x/y on a 5 cm grid with yaw in 90° steps, plus the mat, cord and nightlight states. It is checked for overlaps, chairs within 0.8 m of the table, clear fridge-swing/sink/entrance zones, and a walker-feasible route. Objective = expected toe catches/month from world-model rollouts with common seeds; simulated snags (MuJoCo tables) and brushes only break ties (`TIE_BREAK` 1e-5, `engine.py` and `planner.ts`) and are shown as notes on each option; effort is tracked separately; options that save no toe catches are dropped. B200 job 47577852 (Slurm, 1.5 mm taped edge, glide caps in the action set): 53,019 layouts (50,297 valid), 102,528 evaluations, 10.09 M imagined trips, 1,053 s wall time (41.1 s GPU rollout). Scanned 1.14 toe catches/month; options (confirm seed 2, `planner/check_options.py`, each fix put back alone): **chosen** remove the mat, effort 1, $0 → 0.07 (mat saves 1.06); **fewest** chair5 + chair8 moved, mat removed, cord rerouted, effort 8.9, $6 → 0.00 (mat 1.06, cord 0.07, chairs ~0: they cut brushes 148 → 60); **least effort for 20% fewer** mat removed + cord taped, effort 2, $8 → 0.00. Outputs are in `out/planner/kitchen4_lighter/` (replay, Pareto front, job stats).
- **Critic** (`planner/critic.py`, ONNX, inputs and outputs clamped to the training range). Critic job 47577853, target = the toe-catch objective: held-out 20% (n = 10,059) RMSE 0.01/month, R² 0.999, Spearman 0.90, top-5% overlap 0.24. In the browser it searches thousands of layouts from the judge's layout and confirms the top 5 with real rollouts, so the AI's answer is never worse than the judge's. The OT report's ranked fixes now come from the planner.
- **Gait personalization** (`worldmodel/persona.py`, `viewer/src/gaitfit.ts`, HANDOFF_GAIT.md). 8-d embedding, leave-two-out CV over 14 PhysioNet people. Fitting from walker + head improves head error 9.8 → 5.2 cm (12/14 people) and walker error 12.4 → 11.7 cm (10/14). It **does not improve heel** (4.03 → 4.13 cm), and an oracle test says heel style isn't identifiable from this data; closed-loop results are about neutral.
- **Proof:** `proof/demo/planner.mp4`, `planner_vs_judge.mp4`, `personalization.mp4` (`viewer/e2e/record_planner.ts`); e2e check 13.

- **Live futures fan** (`viewer/src/futuresfan.ts`, `futures.ts` in `fanworker.ts`; panel "Futures fan"; labelled "imagined"):
  - Every 0.5 s it samples 30 futures 4-5 s ahead (adaptive: up to the nearest fork + 1 m) from the walker's current pose. Each future samples the mixture (not the mean).
  - **The model is the trained one.** `worldmodel/fan_parity.py` runs the checkpoint `out/gait/folds/fold6_12_13.pt` in torch on 24 fresh measured states × every style. `tests/futures.test.ts` runs the fan's own path (gaitfit → worldmodel predict) on the same states. Worst output diff is 4.5e-6, and weights match persona.json to 5.0e-7.
  - **Forks** (`routeOptions`): there are alternatives around bump objects ("around the mat") and wide of obstacles (0.25 m keep-out). One counts only if it deviates more than 15 cm and the decision is more than 25 cm ahead. Each future picks a route by p = softmax(−length / 0.5 m). That is an **ASSUMPTION** ("route odds from path length"); the Plan has no cost field. Each route's tip gets a card ("around the mat · 12/30").
