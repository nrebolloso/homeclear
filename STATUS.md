## Walking gait ghosts (branch `juan/futures-gait`, 2026-10-25, not merged)
- **What:** `viewer/src/gaitghosts.ts` + `gaitplan.ts` (run in `fanworker.ts`; panel "Walking ghosts by gait" under World controls; `game.gaitGhosts`). One translucent walker per gait (average foot lift, low-clearance shuffler), each its own colour, walks the planned route. Where it forks (over / around the mat) each gait draws a side with odds from path length + its own catch chance there (`futures.ts` `routeOdds`, `ROUTE_RISK_M`), one draw for both gaits; `configs/demo.json` `gait_seed` 13 sends the average over the mat and the shuffler around it.
